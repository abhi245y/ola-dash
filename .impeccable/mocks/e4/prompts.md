# E4 theme pack concept boards (Ola Dash, unofficial electric-scooter dashboard)

Shared facts for every image: landscape 1280x768 head-unit screen, rendered edge to edge, no device frame, no brand marks, crisp readable text. The top 48 px (6 percent of the height) is the host warning-light strip, drawn by the host above every theme: a thin dark band with real scooter lights only, left to right: left indicator, right indicator, high beam, headlight, READY, lock, side stand, fault, charging; lit ones solid in their status colour (indicators and READY green, high beam blue, side stand amber, fault red), unlit ones faint outlines; a small yellow square hub button at its far right. Nothing else goes in that strip. Everything below it belongs to the theme. Every fact appears once per screen. Numbers shown: speed 42 km/h, mode Sport, battery 68 %, range 54 km, trip 12.4 km, odometer 3,218 km, clock 18:24, battery current 38 A at 52 V (about 2.0 kW), pack temperature 31 C, motor temperature 48 C. Status colours (green, blue, amber, red) belong only to the warning lights; each theme has its own accent.

## Theme 1: Twin Dial (look: "Instrument")
Visual law: deep blue-black glass ground (#0B0E13), very subtle depth, crisp white numerals in a precision condensed technical sans with tabular figures, hairline engraved scales and tick marks, one warm copper-orange accent (#FF7A1A) for needles and focus, kept clearly distinct from amber. Flat enough for sunlight: solid surfaces, strong strokes, no glow, no chrome, no bevels. Layout: a large analog speedometer (0 to 120 km/h, major ticks every 20, needle at 42, the digital speed 42 large in its centre) on the left half; a power/regen dial on the right half (minus 3 kW regen to plus 6 kW, needle at 2.0 kW, regen arc in a cool tone); between or below them two small dials: battery (68 %) and temperature (pack 31 C); one slim line along the bottom with mode SPORT, range 54 km, trip 12.4 km, clock 18:24.

## Theme 2: Terminal
Visual law: pure black ground, one monospace font, phosphor green text (#33FF66) with a dim green for labels, no other colours outside the strip; readout lines like a system monitor (SPD, BAT, RNG, MODE, PWR, TEMP, TRIP, ODO, TIME) with values right-aligned in fixed columns, ASCII bar gauges built from block characters for speed, battery and power, a prompt line at the bottom with one blinking block cursor, very faint horizontal scanlines. Big speed: the speed value is drawn large in the same monospace font (about a quarter of the screen height) as the hero, the rest stays small but readable at a glance.

## Theme 3: Zen
Visual law: for bright sunlight: warm off-white ground (#F4F1EA), near-black ink (#111111), one quiet accent for the battery bar (deep teal #0F6E6E). Only two facts: speed and battery. Speed is enormous (digits about 60 percent of the screen height), centred, with a small "km/h" beside it; battery is a single thick horizontal bar along the bottom third with "68 %" at its end. Nothing else: no clock, no range, no mode, no decoration. Generous margins, perfect alignment.
